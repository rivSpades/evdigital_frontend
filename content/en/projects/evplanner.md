EvPlanner brings the month's budget, the debts you need to pay and your savings goals
together in one place. It is for people who want to understand where their money goes
without relying on spreadsheets.

Onboarding is guided: the app asks its questions in the right order and only shows
numbers once it has the context to explain them. Someone using it for the first time
understands what they are looking at.

It was built end to end, from the data model to the interface. It is in production and
publicly available, and it is the project that best shows how we work: decide the
essentials first, ship, and keep improving with real use.
